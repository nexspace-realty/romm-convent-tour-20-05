Buying freehold as a foreigner

The title is in your name

  • Foreigners can own up to 49% of a condominium's floor area freehold. These units sit within that foreign quota.
  • No nominee, no lease term: the title deed is registered to you at the Land Office.
  • Funds arrive from abroad with a Foreign Exchange Transaction (FET) form, which we help you arrange.
  • A completed resale unit means no off-plan wait, and a freehold title you can sell again later.

The developer

Built by PROUD Real Estate

Romm Convent is developed by PROUD Real Estate PLC, a Thai developer founded in 1989 and listed on the Market for Alternative Investment (mai) of the Stock Exchange of Thailand since 2004. PROUD builds wellness-focused luxury residences in Bangkok, Hua Hin and Phuket. Romm Convent was created in partnership with BNH Hospital, its neighbour on Convent Road.
